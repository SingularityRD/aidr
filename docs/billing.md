# Billing and enterprise orders

The intended commercial model has two separate channels: online payment for cloud-hosted customers, and enterprise quote/invoice for customers including on-premises deployments. Today, only the enterprise invoice request intake is exposed by default; self-service cloud payment remains disabled until its provider and settlement lifecycle passes acceptance. The enterprise invoice/order workflow uses the finance-approved order and payment ledger as its source of truth; the dashboard only accepts tenant-bound requests for additional protected agents. A request is stored as `pending_finance`; it is **not** a paid invoice and does not change the agent entitlement.

The `invoice-entitlement` Edge function and `enterprise_invoice_orders` table accept idempotent requests from an active tenant owner/admin after session and MFA checks. A repeated request ID with changed terms is rejected. Finance has not yet supplied an approved SKU, signed order, provider-of-record ledger, payment receipt or reconciliation. No production flow in this repository may infer payment from a browser request or grant seats from a pending order. Direct SQL changes to `entitlements` are not a supported sales workflow.

An audited Finance-approval transition now records a paid decision and updates the tenant's agent entitlement atomically. This is a local implementation, not proof of a sold or paid lifecycle: no real provider payment, signed customer order, approved SKU or accounting reconciliation has been accepted. Paid seats alone do not admit a tenant or convert a cloud trial into a paid license; a separately authorized operator must grant a finite licensed admission after verified settlement. No automatic payment-to-license transition has been accepted. Renewal, reduction, cancellation, refund and offboarding must be reconciled and tested against both cloud and self-hosted deployments before sale. Signed entitlement snapshots and the ingest agent-seat limit remain enforcement components, not payment evidence.

Optional Stripe checkout/webhook code exists behind a default-disabled self-service gate. The webhook has local signature/replay tests, but no live account, approved price, webhook registration, portal, invoice retrieval, refund/cancellation or provider reconciliation acceptance. Paddle helpers are legacy and stay disabled. The dashboard does not show invented prices, exchange rates or an empty invoice ledger as a successful lookup.

The implementation boundaries and acceptance scenarios are recorded in [billing-v6.md](production-readiness/billing-v6.md) and the [commercial evidence registry](production-readiness/commercial-go-evidence-registry.json). **T33 and commercial billing approval remain open.**
