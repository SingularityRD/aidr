# First-customer-ready: pre-customer release profile

**Decision today: NO-GO for a live pilot offer or production deployment.** This is a pre-customer gate: it can be completed using AIDR-owned accounts, devices, infrastructure, operators, and independent reviewers. A named customer, customer-specific IdP/MDM, signed order, customer PoV, and customer acceptance are **not** prerequisites for this gate. They remain prerequisites for the relevant customer deployment and the separate direct-GA decision.

This profile defines when a **scoped, supported evaluation offer may be considered**; it does not authorize an offer today. Authorization requires evidence that every FCR-01–08 criterion passed for the exact candidate, the named decision owners signed a candidate-specific pre-customer GO, and the proposed scope meets the FCR security, legal, privacy and operations criteria below. Customer-dependent evaluation receipts authorize the subsequent customer pilot; they are not evidence for this pre-customer decision. **The current decision remains NO-GO**, so do not make a live pilot or evaluation offer until that decision changes. This does not authorize general availability, an unrestricted production sale, all connectors, all regions, a certified security outcome, or a 99.9% SLA. The [commercial registry](./commercial-go-evidence-registry.json) and its 13 [hard-gate receipts](./commercial-gate-receipts.json) remain `NO-GO`/`open` until their own criteria are met. Do not relabel those gates to make this profile pass. The manually maintained [readiness backlog](../../READINESS_BACKLOG.json) is an issue ledger; its baseline and verification snapshots are historical, not evidence for this checkout. The generated [feature inventory](../../FEATURE_INVENTORY.json) and [sales-gap map](../../PRODUCTION_SALES_GAPS.json) describe this worktree but are not release proof. As inspected on **24 September 2026**, the [candidate evidence index](../../.release-evidence/v8-evidence-index.json) has no candidate digest and all 30 gates are `NOT_RUN`; only a newly generated, candidate-bound index can support release acceptance. The commercial registry is the release decision record.

## What may be offered

| Stage | Permitted once its proof exists | Still prohibited |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introductory discussion | Show architecture, known limitations, and the [synthetic demo](./controlled-demo-runbook.md), clearly labelled as synthetic. | Claim the demo proves detection effectiveness, host side-effect prevention, customer fit, production readiness, or a supported release. |
| First-customer-ready controlled evaluation | Offer **self-hosted and managed-cloud paths**, each rehearsed end to end in AIDR-owned environments, with a named region, connector/host/OS/version set, feature set, data-flow set, and evaluation period for each. Give the buyer a draft Evaluation Charter, support contact, stop/rollback procedure, and limitations sheet. | Enable real customer data, start a production deployment, promise a paid entitlement, support/SLA, or expand to untested connectors/regions without customer-specific approvals and acceptance. |
| Customer pilot/production | Requires the buyer's environment, consent, legal/privacy/security review, signed terms, owner matrix, and independent customer PoV/acceptance for the exact signed candidate. | Treat an AIDR-owned rehearsal as customer acceptance or as closure of the direct-GA hard gates. |

This profile requires **both** a self-hosted staging topology and an AIDR-owned managed-cloud staging project, plus **only connectors whose real host rows pass**. A missing host is an exclusion, not a pass. Optional MCP gateway, AIG, cloud/MDM discovery, SSO/SCIM, SIEM, MDM fleet rollout, endpoint isolation, billing automation, multi-region residency, and air-gap delivery are excluded unless separately proven and written into the proposed scope. Keeping an option out of scope is better than inventing its acceptance.

## GO checklist for a controlled evaluation offer

Every applicable row needs a **PASS receipt for the same clean source commit and release inventory digest**, with date, operator, environment, tool/host versions, redacted logs, reviewer, and defect disposition. A failed/unknown/unavailable row stays open. Evidence must be generated, not copied from an older candidate. As inspected on 5 October 2026, `.release-evidence/v8-evidence-index.json` is absent from this checkout. No candidate-bound gate status is available; local component test passes do not change this decision.

| ID | AIDR-owned proof before the first live pilot offer | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FCR-01 — candidate and trust | Verify the committed AIG vendor tree against its owned-source pin, build from a clean tree, produce an immutable inventory/SBOM/provenance, sign with an approved publisher identity held outside the bundle, and verify on a second machine using an independently delivered trust policy. Exercise tamper, expiry, wrong signer, and rollback rejection. | Unsigned package, dirty source, mismatched digest, or unowned signing key. |
| FCR-02 — baseline engineering | On that candidate run type/static checks, unit/integration suites, production dependency/secret scans, canonical migration checks, package-consumer installation, release build, and evidence-index verification. Preserve failures, including known skips, rather than changing the denominator. | Required test failure, unresolved high/critical dependency issue, secret leak, or gate without a candidate digest. |
| FCR-03 — real installation | In AIDR-owned infrastructure with real DNS/TLS, deploy **both** self-hosted and managed-cloud topologies from the signed package; on **each** run actual migrations, bootstrap a test tenant, login with an enabled method, enroll a fresh device through device-start/poll, observe `managed.json`, publish policy, ingest an event, view/export it in the browser, update, uninstall, and recover from a failed upgrade. Repeat from clean machine/project state. | Loopback-only/static proof, unreachable endpoint, placeholder credentials, or manual source-tree repair on either path. |
| FCR-04 — host enforcement | For **each connector/version to be offered**, install the licensed real host on each listed OS/architecture and run benign twin, safe deny canary, host-side effect observation, disabled/crashed/timed-out hook, offline/restart, update and rollback. Record attempted and absent/present side effects independently of the connector's returned decision. Publish the supported matrix and known bypasses. | Version probe, fixture-only test, missing host, unobserved side effect, or unqualified blanket protection claim. |
| FCR-05 — security and data | Triage all full-history secret-scan findings with a named Security owner; revoke/rotate any live secrets. Test cross-tenant negative access, credential revocation, signing boundaries, egress destinations/fields, log/support-bundle redaction, retention/deletion and backup expiry on a disposable two-tenant deployment. Obtain a scoped independent security review before handling real customer data; fix/retest high and critical issues. | Untriaged historical secret, tenant escape, unapproved outbound data, or unresolved high/critical finding. |
| FCR-06 — recoverable operations | For **both** paths use AIDR-owned off-host storage and separately controlled recovery material or provider-managed recovery controls with independently verified restore access. Run monitored backup schedules; restore into another machine/project/failure domain, compare tenant records and objects, measure actual RPO/RTO, and test failed upload/restore alarms. Name the on-call operator, escalation path, ticket channel, and incident stop procedure; perform a timestamped support drill. | Same-host-only restore, shared key/backup failure domain, unmonitored scheduler, or no reachable human support on either path. |
| FCR-07 — scale and failure | Declare the proposed pilot limit (devices, action rate, tenants, retention) for **both** paths; run an authenticated representative workload for at least eight hours on each topology, record p50/p95/p99/errors/loss and two-tenant noisy-neighbour results, and rehearse node/DB/network failure. Set conservative pilot limits from each path's measured headroom. **Do not advertise an SLA from these runs.** | Historical/shared-process noisy-neighbour failure remains unexplained, data loss is silent, or target exceeds measured headroom on either path. |
| FCR-08 — buyer-safe packet | Publish exact supported scope/exclusions, install/rollback guide, data-flow and subprocessors for **enabled** paths, pilot terms/DPA/retention and responsibility split reviewed by Legal/Privacy, a real contact/status route, pricing marked "by approved order" until Finance approves it, and an Evaluation Charter template. Crawl the deployed demo and contact/legal links. Claims must match the candidate and [claim registry](./commercial-go-evidence-registry.json). | Missing owner, broken route, unsupported compliance/performance/coverage claim, or implied customer testimonial. |

Use existing repository checks and runbooks rather than a new gate framework: [external gate runner](./external-gates-runbook.md), [first-customer installation guide](../../deploy/FIRST_CUSTOMER.md), [native matrix](../../deploy/native/support-matrix.json), [commercial packet](./first-customer-sales-packet-v8.md), and [controlled demo](./controlled-demo-runbook.md). The release operator records the FCR-01–08 receipts in a candidate-specific review, signs the decision with Product, QA, Security, Operations, Privacy/Legal and Support, and records excluded capabilities. **A document saying PASS without linked receipts is NO-GO.**

## Customer-dependent work is deferred, not erased

After first contact, collect the buyer's actual host builds, IdP, MDM/cloud scopes, region, network, retention, workload, legal entity, support expectations and procurement requirements. Before any real customer pilot, obtain written authorization and a scoped Evaluation Charter; then repeat the applicable host, privacy, installation, restore and failure cases in that environment. Before production or a paid GA sale, complete the relevant [technical field acceptance rows](../../PRODUCTION_SALES_GAPS.json), commercial hard gates, external assessment, customer acceptance and exact-candidate release decision. Until then describe the result as **a candidate for controlled evaluation**, never as production-ready.
