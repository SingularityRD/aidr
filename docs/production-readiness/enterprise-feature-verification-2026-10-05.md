# Enterprise feature verification — 5 October 2026

## Current source and local verification

The earlier inspection below is historical. Its OIDC, syslog transport, SCIM rate-limit, retention-management and SAML policy-administration gaps have been superseded by the implementation and checks in this section, updated through 6 October. **No deployed environment, live IdP, live SIEM or customer host was involved.** Source, handler, socket and disposable-database tests establish local behavior; they do not establish customer or production acceptance.

These capabilities belong to the Supabase control plane in `aidr`. The managed app in `aidr-landing` uses Clerk, Firestore and Polar. Its optional organization path now uses an immutable reciprocal organization/tenant binding and fresh native Clerk organization membership; selected-directory enforcement still lacks an authoritative per-user directory grant and remains unavailable under its strict guard. Supabase control-plane capability must not be presented as managed SaaS capability; both deployment paths require their own acceptance evidence.
