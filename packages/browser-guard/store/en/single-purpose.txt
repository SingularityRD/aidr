Browser Guard has a single purpose: keep personal and confidential data out of what the user sends to AI chat services. On supported AI chat sites (and company-internal AI portals an administrator adds) it checks prompts, pastes and uploads before they are sent, replaces sensitive values with placeholders, and restores the originals locally in the answers. Its other functions serve that purpose: blocking AI services an administrator does not allow, and reporting counts of what was masked to the organisation's own control plane when one is configured.
